# Price Match Guarantee

## Our Commitment

BuildRight Hardware offers a **Price Match Guarantee**. If you find a lower price on an identical, in-stock item at a qualifying Canadian competitor, we will match it — both in store and online. We also match our own prices for a limited time after you buy (see [Post-Purchase Price Protection](#post-purchase-price-protection)).

Our goal is simple: you should never have to choose between BuildRight service and the best price.

## What We Match

We will match the advertised price of identical items (same brand, model number, colour, and size) sold and shipped by:

- Major Canadian hardware and home-improvement retailers (physical stores and their official websites)
- National department stores and warehouse clubs with a hardware section, where the item is available to the general public
- Our own buildright.ca price if it drops after your purchase, within the protection window

## Conditions for Price Match

To qualify for a price match:

1. The item must be **identical** — same brand, model number, and specifications. Comparable or substitute models do not qualify.
2. The competitor's price must be **currently advertised** and verifiable (printed flyer, website, or in-app price).
3. The competitor's item must be **in stock** and available for immediate purchase.
4. The competitor must be a **Canadian retailer** with the price listed in CAD.
5. Price matches are limited to **one per customer per identical item**, and to reasonable household quantities.
6. The price must reflect the total comparable cost, including the competitor's standard shipping for online comparisons.

## What We Do Not Match

We do not match prices from:

- Third-party marketplace sellers (e.g., individual sellers on Amazon, eBay, Facebook Marketplace, or Kijiji)
- Liquidation, clearance, going-out-of-business, or "open-box" outlets
- Auctions, daily flash deals, doorcrasher/limited-quantity loss leaders, and "while supplies last" offers
- Prices that are the result of an obvious pricing or typographical error
- Bundle deals, gift-with-purchase, or package promotions where the item cannot be purchased separately
- Prices requiring a membership, coupon stacking, financing offer, or loyalty card the general public cannot access
- International retailers, U.S. stores, or cross-border prices
- Black Friday, Boxing Day, and other doorcrasher event pricing that is explicitly excluded in the competitor's terms
- Our own clearance, final-sale, and special-order items

## How to Request a Price Match

### In Store

Show the advertised price (printed flyer or on your phone) to a store associate at the time of purchase. The associate will verify the price and apply the match immediately. We can usually confirm a competitor's online price right at the service desk.

### Online (At Time of Purchase)

Start a chat at buildright.ca before completing your order, share a link or screenshot of the competitor's price, and our team will adjust your cart if it qualifies.

### After Purchase

If you find a lower qualifying price **within 7 days** of your purchase, contact us through our website chat or customer service line with proof of the competitor's price (or a link). We will refund the difference to your original payment method.

## Post-Purchase Price Protection

If BuildRight Hardware lowers the price of an item you bought within the last **30 days**, we will refund the difference. Bring your receipt to any store or contact us online — BuildRight Rewards members are automatically eligible and can request the adjustment from their order history. Price protection excludes clearance markdowns, limited-time doorcrasher events, and special orders.

## Price Match on Sale Items

Our price match guarantee also applies to items that are on sale at a qualifying competitor, as long as the sale is currently active, the item is in stock, and the offer is not on the exclusions list above.

## Combining with Other Offers

A price match reflects the competitor's price in place of any BuildRight promotion on that item; it cannot be stacked with additional BuildRight coupons or promo codes on the same item. You will still **earn BuildRight Rewards points** on the price you pay. See the [Rewards Program](rewards-program.md) for details.
