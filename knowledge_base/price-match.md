# Price Match Guarantee

## Our Commitment

BuildRight Hardware offers a **Price Match Guarantee**. If you find a lower price on an identical item at a qualifying competitor, we will match it — in-store and online.

## What We Match

We will match the price of identical items (same brand, model number, colour, and size) sold and shipped by the following types of competitors:
- Major Canadian hardware and home improvement retailers (physical stores and their official websites)
- National department stores with a hardware section

## Conditions for Price Match

To qualify for a price match:
1. The item must be identical (same brand, model, and specifications).
2. The competitor's price must be **currently advertised** (not expired sales or flash deals).
3. The competitor's item must be **in stock** and available for immediate purchase.
4. The competitor must be a **Canadian retailer**.
5. Price matches are **one per customer per identical item**.

## What We Do Not Match

We do not match prices from:
- Third-party marketplace sellers (e.g., individual sellers on Amazon, eBay)
- Liquidation or clearance outlets
- Prices that are the result of a pricing error
- Bundle deals or package promotions where the item cannot be purchased separately
- Prices requiring a membership or loyalty card
- International retailers or U.S. stores

## How to Request a Price Match

### In Store
Show the advertised price (print or on your phone) to a store associate at the time of purchase. The associate will verify the price and apply the match immediately.

### Online / After Purchase
If you find a lower price **within 7 days** of your purchase, contact us through our website chat or customer service line with proof of the competitor's price. We will refund the difference to your original payment method.

## Price Match on Sale Items

Our price match guarantee also applies to items that are on sale at a qualifying competitor, as long as the sale is currently active and the item is in stock.
