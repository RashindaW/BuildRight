# Order Tracking & Changes

## Overview

This page explains how to track your BuildRight Hardware order, change or cancel it before it ships, update a delivery address, and what to do about delays, missing items, or damage. For shipping rates and timelines, see [Shipping & Delivery](shipping-delivery.md).

## Tracking Your Order

### Online

1. Sign in at buildright.ca and go to **My Orders**.
2. Select your order to see its status: **Processing → Ready/Shipped → Out for Delivery → Delivered** (or **Ready for Pickup** for in-store pickup).
3. Once shipped, your **tracking number** links directly to the carrier's tracking page.

### By Email and SMS

- You receive an **order confirmation** immediately after placing the order.
- You receive a **shipped** or **ready for pickup** notification with tracking when your order is fulfilled.
- Opt in to **SMS updates** at checkout for text notifications at each step.

### Guest Orders

If you checked out as a guest, use the **Track Order** link in your confirmation email, or enter your order number and email on the **Track Order** page at buildright.ca.

## Order Status Explained

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Processing | Payment confirmed; we are preparing your order |
| Ready for Pickup | Your in-store pickup order is ready (held 7 days) |
| Shipped | Your order is with the carrier; tracking is active |
| Out for Delivery | Arriving today |
| Delivered | Carrier marked the order delivered |
| Backordered | An item is temporarily out of stock; you'll get an updated date |
| Cancelled | The order (or an item) was cancelled and refunded if charged |

## Modifying or Cancelling an Order

You can modify or cancel an order **before it ships**.

- **How:** go to **My Orders**, select the order, and choose **Cancel** or **Edit** if the option is available, or contact us right away at 1-800-BUILD-IT or support@buildright.ca.
- **Timing:** orders placed before 2 PM local time often ship the same day, so contact us as soon as possible. Once an order is in **Shipped** status, it can no longer be changed and you'll need the [Returns Policy](returns.md).
- **Refunds for cancellations:** if you were already charged, the refund follows the [Refund Policy Details](refund-policy-details.md) timelines.
- **Special orders:** cancellations may forfeit the 20% non-refundable deposit (see [Store Policies › Special Orders](store-policies.md)).

### Changing Items or Quantities

If you need a different quantity, size, or model, the fastest route once an order is being processed is usually to **cancel and reorder**, or to call us so we can adjust before fulfillment.

## Changing a Delivery Address

- **Before shipment:** we can usually update the shipping address — contact us immediately with your order number.
- **After shipment:** address changes may be possible directly with the carrier using your tracking number, but cannot be guaranteed. If a package is undeliverable and returned to us, we will refund or reship.
- **In-store pickup location:** if you chose the wrong store, contact us before the order is ready and we will try to redirect it, or cancel and reorder at the correct store.

## Adding an Alternate Pickup Person

For in-store pickup, you can authorize someone else to collect your order:

- Add an **alternate pickup person** in your order details, or notify us by phone.
- They must bring the **order number/confirmation** and a piece of **photo ID** matching the name on file.

## Delivery Windows

- **Standard and express** shipments are delivered during the carrier's normal delivery hours; exact times are not guaranteed.
- **Freight and major appliance** deliveries are scheduled with a **3–4 hour window**, booked at checkout or by phone (see [Shipping & Delivery › Major Appliance Delivery](shipping-delivery.md)).
- You will receive notifications as your delivery window approaches.

## Delays

If your order is taking longer than expected:

- **Check tracking first** in **My Orders** for the latest carrier update.
- **No movement for 5 business days:** contact us so we can open a carrier investigation.
- **Backordered items:** we will email an updated availability date; you may keep the order, split the shipment, or cancel the backordered item for a refund.
- **Weather and holidays:** severe weather and statutory holidays can extend transit times; see [Shipping & Delivery › Statutory Holidays](shipping-delivery.md).

## Missing Items or Wrong Item

- **Partial delivery:** orders may ship in **multiple boxes** or from different locations and arrive separately — check your shipment details before reporting an item missing.
- **Still missing or incorrect:** report it within **5 business days** via **My Orders › Report a Problem** or by calling 1-800-BUILD-IT. We will send the correct item or issue a refund.
- **Marked delivered but not received:** contact us; we will investigate with the carrier and reship or refund if it cannot be located (see [Shipping & Delivery › Lost or Delayed Shipments](shipping-delivery.md)).

## Damaged Items

- **Refuse visibly damaged deliveries** at the door where possible, and **note damage on the delivery receipt** for freight/appliances before signing.
- Report damage within **5 business days** with photos of the item and packaging.
- Keep all packaging until the claim is resolved. See the [Returns Policy › Damaged or Defective Items](returns.md).
