# Frequently Asked Questions

## Store Policies

### What are your store hours?
BuildRight Hardware stores are open Monday–Friday 7 AM–9 PM, Saturday 7 AM–8 PM, and Sunday 8 AM–6 PM (local time). Hours may vary by location and statutory holidays.

### Do you offer installation services?
Yes. We offer professional installation for select products including appliances, smart home devices, and outdoor power equipment. Visit any store location or check the product page online to see if installation is available and to book a time.

### Can I order online and pick up in store?
Yes. Most items are available for free in-store pickup, sometimes as soon as same-day for orders placed before 2 PM. You will receive a notification when your order is ready.

## Products

### Do you carry Mastercraft brand products?
Yes. Mastercraft is one of our key brands for tools, hardware, and outdoor equipment. We carry the full Mastercraft lineup including power tools, hand tools, and storage solutions.

### Do you sell propane tanks?
Yes. We carry 20 lb propane tanks and related accessories (regulators, hoses) in-store. Note that propane tanks cannot be shipped and must be picked up in store.

### Can I get a product recommendation?
Our in-store associates are knowledgeable about all product categories and can help you choose the right tool or product for your project. You can also ask our Store Assistant chatbot for quick product guidance anytime.

## Orders and Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit cards, and cash (in-store only). Online orders can be paid by credit or debit card.

### Can I modify or cancel my order?
Orders can be modified or cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, you will need to use our return process. Contact customer service as soon as possible to request a cancellation or change.

### Is it safe to pay online?
Yes. Our online store uses industry-standard SSL encryption and secure payment processing. We do not store your full card number.

## Loyalty and Promotions

### Do you have a loyalty program?
Yes. The BuildRight Rewards program lets you earn points on every purchase. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases. Sign up in-store or online for free.

### How do I use a promotional code?
Enter your promotional code in the "Promo Code" field at checkout. The discount will be applied automatically. Codes cannot be combined with other offers unless stated.
