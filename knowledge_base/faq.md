# Frequently Asked Questions

## Store Policies

### What are your store hours?
Smart Handy Man stores are open Monday–Friday 7 AM–9 PM, Saturday 7 AM–8 PM, and Sunday 8 AM–6 PM (local time). Hours may vary by location and on statutory holidays. Check your local store page at smarthandyman.ca for holiday hours.

### Do you offer installation services?
Yes. We offer professional installation for select products including appliances, smart-home devices, and outdoor power equipment. We also offer in-store and in-home assembly for items like BBQs, furniture, and shelving. See [Assembly & Installation](assembly-and-installation.md) for what's included and how to book.

### Can I order online and pick up in store?
Yes. Most items are available for free in-store pickup, sometimes as soon as same-day for orders placed before 2 PM. You will receive a notification when your order is ready, and we hold orders for 7 days. See [Shipping & Delivery › Free In-Store Pickup](shipping-delivery.md).

### What is your return policy?
You have **90 days** to return most items (120 days for BuildRight Rewards members). Defective or damaged items should be reported within **30 days** of delivery. See the [Returns Policy](returns.md) for full details.

### Do you price match?
Yes. We match identical, in-stock items from qualifying Canadian competitors, and we offer 30-day price protection on our own prices. See our [Price Match Guarantee](price-match.md).

## Products

### Do you carry Mastercraft brand products?
Yes. Mastercraft is one of our key house brands for tools, hardware, and outdoor equipment. We carry the full Mastercraft lineup including power tools, hand tools, and storage solutions. Our other house brands are ProBuilt, IronClad, VoltEdge, and AquaFlow.

### What are BuildRight's house brands?
- **Mastercraft** — hand and power tools, storage, outdoor equipment
- **ProBuilt** — professional-grade power tools and the ProBuilt cordless platform
- **IronClad** — hand tools and hardware with a lifetime limited warranty
- **VoltEdge** — batteries, chargers, and cordless power solutions
- **AquaFlow** — plumbing fixtures, faucets, valves, and water heaters

### Do you sell propane tanks?
Yes. We carry 20 lb propane tanks and related accessories (regulators, hoses) in store. Propane tanks cannot be shipped and must be picked up in store, and purchasers must be 18 or older. See [Safety & Recalls](safety-and-recalls.md).

### Can I get a product recommendation?
Our in-store associates are knowledgeable about every category and can help you choose the right tool or product for your project. You can also ask our Store Assistant chatbot at smarthandyman.ca for quick product guidance anytime.

### Do your cordless tools share batteries?
Yes. ProBuilt and VoltEdge cordless tools run on the shared VoltEdge battery platform, so one battery works across compatible tools. Batteries and chargers carry a 3-year warranty.

### How do I care for my tools and equipment?
See [Product Care & Maintenance](product-care-and-maintenance.md) for battery storage, blade care, paint storage, and seasonal winterization guides.

## Orders and Payments

### What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit cards, the BuildRight Credit Card, and cash (in store only). Online orders can be paid by credit or debit card, BuildRight Credit Card, or BuildRight gift card.

### Can I modify or cancel my order?
Orders can be modified or cancelled before they are shipped. Once shipped, you will need to use our return process. Contact customer service as soon as possible — see [Order Tracking & Changes](order-tracking-and-changes.md).

### How do I track my order?
Log in at smarthandyman.ca and go to **My Orders**, or use the tracking number emailed to you when your order ships. See [Order Tracking & Changes](order-tracking-and-changes.md).

### Is it safe to pay online?
Yes. Our online store uses industry-standard SSL encryption and secure payment processing. We do not store your full card number.

### Do you offer financing?
Yes. The BuildRight Credit Card offers equal-payment and deferred-payment financing plans on qualifying purchases. See [Financing & Credit](financing-and-credit.md).

### How much is shipping?
Standard shipping is $7.99, or **free on orders over $49**. Express is $14.99. Oversized and freight items are quoted at checkout. See [Shipping & Delivery](shipping-delivery.md).

## Loyalty and Promotions

### Do you have a loyalty program?
Yes. **BuildRight Rewards** lets you earn 1 point per $1 spent, with 500 points = $5 in reward credit. Sign up in store or online for free. See the [Rewards Program](rewards-program.md).

### How do I use a promotional code?
Enter your promo code in the **Promo Code** field at checkout. The discount is applied automatically. Codes cannot be combined with other offers unless stated.

### Do you sell gift cards?
Yes. Gift cards are available in $25, $50, $100, and $250 denominations, never expire, and can be used in store or online. See [Gift Cards](gift-cards.md).

### Do you have accounts for contractors and businesses?
Yes. We offer volume pricing, dedicated reps, net-30 terms, and priority processing through our Commercial and Pro accounts. See [Commercial & Pro Accounts](commercial-and-pro-accounts.md).

## Safety and Recalls

### How do I find out about product recalls?
We post active recalls at smarthandyman.ca and notify affected customers we can identify (including BuildRight Rewards members). See [Safety & Recalls](safety-and-recalls.md) for the recall process and how to return a recalled item.

### Where can I find a Safety Data Sheet (SDS)?
SDS documents for chemicals and hazardous products are available on the product page online and from any store associate. See [Safety & Recalls › Safety Data Sheets](safety-and-recalls.md).
