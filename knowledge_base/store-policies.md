# Store Policies

## Privacy and Data

BuildRight Hardware respects your privacy. We collect only the information needed to process your orders and improve your shopping experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

For full details, see our Privacy Policy on the website.

## Age Restrictions

Certain products (propane tanks, some chemicals, certain power tools) require customers to be 18 or older to purchase. Proof of age may be requested in store or at delivery.

## Loyalty Program

### Earning Points
- Earn 1 point per $1 spent on qualifying purchases.
- Bonus point events are announced in-store and via email.
- Points do not expire as long as your account has activity within 12 months.

### Redeeming Points
- 500 points = $5 reward credit.
- Credits can be applied at checkout in-store or online.
- Credits cannot be applied to the purchase of gift cards or propane tanks.

## Gift Cards

BuildRight Hardware gift cards are available in-store and online in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $250. They do not expire and can be used in-store or online. Lost or stolen cards cannot be replaced.

## Special Orders

Items not in regular stock can be special-ordered. Standard lead time is 5–10 business days. Special orders require a 20% non-refundable deposit. Once received, special-order items are subject to the standard 90-day return policy for defects only (not change-of-mind).

## Bulk and Commercial Accounts

We offer volume pricing for contractors and businesses. Contact our commercial sales team for account setup and pricing tiers. Commercial accounts have access to dedicated service reps and priority order processing.

## Safety Policies

### Propane and Hazardous Materials
- Propane tanks and pressurized containers must be handled and stored per local regulations.
- Flammable items (aerosols, paint thinners) cannot be shipped; in-store pickup only.
- Customers are responsible for following all safety instructions on product labels.

### Power Tool Demonstrations
- In-store power tool demonstrations are conducted by trained associates only.
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) is required in designated workshop areas.

## Accessibility

BuildRight Hardware is committed to accessible service. All stores are wheelchair accessible. For assistance with online accessibility, contact us at accessibility@buildright.ca.

## Code of Conduct

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone who is verbally abusive, harassing, or threatening to staff or other customers.
