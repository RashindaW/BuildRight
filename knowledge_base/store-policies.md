# Store Policies

## Overview

This page summarizes BuildRight Hardware's general store policies. Several topics have their own detailed pages, which are linked from each section below. For shopping policies like returns, refunds, warranty, shipping, and price matching, see those dedicated documents.

## Privacy and Data

BuildRight Hardware respects your privacy. We collect only the information needed to process your orders, operate the BuildRight Rewards program, and improve your shopping experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties, and we comply with Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA and applicable provincial legislation).

- You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at privacy@buildright.ca.
- Marketing emails include an unsubscribe link; transactional messages (order updates) are sent regardless of marketing preferences.
- For full details, see our Privacy Policy at buildright.ca.

## Payment and Pricing

- We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit, the BuildRight Credit Card, cash (in store), and BuildRight gift cards.
- All prices are in **CAD** and exclusive of tax. Applicable **GST/HST/PST** is added at checkout based on your province.
- In the rare event of a pricing error, we reserve the right to correct it and will honour a reasonable resolution.
- Financing is available through the [BuildRight Credit Card](financing-and-credit.md).

## Age Restrictions

Certain products require customers to be **18 or older** to purchase, including propane and fuel, some chemicals and solvents, and certain power tools and power-actuated tools. Proof of age may be requested in store or at delivery. See [Safety & Recalls › Age-Restricted Items](safety-and-recalls.md).

## Loyalty Program

BuildRight Rewards lets you earn **1 point per $1** spent on qualifying purchases, with **500 points = $5** in reward credit. Points do not expire as long as your account has activity within **12 months**. Reward credits cannot be applied to gift cards or propane. Full earning rates, tiers, bonus events, and exclusions are in the [Rewards Program](rewards-program.md).

## Gift Cards

BuildRight Hardware gift cards are available in store and online in denominations of **$25, $50, $100, and $250**. They do not expire and can be used in store or online. Lost or stolen physical cards cannot be replaced. Bulk and corporate orders are available. See [Gift Cards](gift-cards.md) for balance checks and full terms.

## Special Orders

Items not in regular stock can be special-ordered.

- Standard lead time is **5–10 business days** (some items longer; you will be quoted at the time of order).
- Special orders require a **20% non-refundable deposit**.
- Once received, special-order items are subject to the standard 90-day return policy **for defects only**, not change of mind.
- Large or custom special orders may be coordinated through a [Commercial or Pro account](commercial-and-pro-accounts.md).

## Bulk and Commercial Accounts

We offer **volume pricing** for contractors and businesses. Commercial accounts include dedicated service reps, **net-30** terms, priority order processing, and bulk/special-order support. Contact our commercial sales team to set up an account. See [Commercial & Pro Accounts](commercial-and-pro-accounts.md) for tiers and benefits.

## Safety Policies

### Propane and Hazardous Materials

- Propane tanks and pressurized containers must be handled and stored per local regulations.
- Flammable items (aerosols, paint thinners, certain adhesives) cannot be shipped; in-store pickup only.
- Customers are responsible for following all safety instructions on product labels.
- Safety Data Sheets (SDS) are available online and in store.

See [Safety & Recalls](safety-and-recalls.md) for recalls, SDS access, PPE, and shipping restrictions.

### Power Tool Demonstrations

- In-store power tool demonstrations are conducted by trained associates only.
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) is required in designated workshop and demo areas.

## Order Changes and Tracking

Orders can be modified or cancelled before they ship, and you can track shipments from **My Orders** at buildright.ca. For address changes, delays, and missing or damaged items, see [Order Tracking & Changes](order-tracking-and-changes.md).

## Accessibility

BuildRight Hardware is committed to accessible service in line with provincial accessibility legislation (such as the AODA in Ontario).

- All stores are wheelchair accessible, with accessible parking and washrooms.
- Service animals are welcome.
- For assistance with online accessibility, contact accessibility@buildright.ca.

## Environmental and Recycling Programs

BuildRight Hardware participates in stewardship and recycling programs across Canada:

- **Battery, paint, and electronics recycling** drop-off is available at most stores, in line with provincial programs.
- **Propane tank exchange** keeps old cylinders in safe circulation.
- We accept **used lead-acid batteries** for recycling, often with a core credit.

Availability of specific programs depends on provincial regulations and your local store.

## Code of Conduct

We reserve the right to refuse service to anyone who is verbally abusive, harassing, or threatening to staff or other customers. We are committed to a respectful, safe environment for everyone in our stores and on our channels.
