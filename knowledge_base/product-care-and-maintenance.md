# Product Care & Maintenance

## Overview

Caring for your tools and equipment keeps them safe, extends their life, and protects your warranty — many warranty claims are denied for damage caused by missed maintenance. This guide covers power tools and batteries, blades and bits, paint storage, outdoor equipment winterization, and seasonal plumbing care. When in doubt, follow the manufacturer's manual, which takes precedence over these general tips.

For warranty terms, see the [Warranty Policy](warranty.md).

## Power Tool & Battery Care

### VoltEdge / ProBuilt Lithium-Ion Batteries

- **Store at room temperature**, ideally between **10°C and 25°C**, away from direct sunlight, moisture, and metal objects that could short the terminals.
- **Avoid freezing.** Never charge a battery that is below **0°C**; let it warm to room temperature first. Charging a frozen cell can permanently damage it.
- **Store partially charged.** For long-term storage (over a month), keep batteries at roughly **40–60%** charge, not fully empty or fully full.
- **Recharge every 3 months** during storage to prevent deep discharge.
- **Use the matched charger.** VoltEdge batteries are designed for VoltEdge chargers; off-brand chargers can void the 3-year warranty.
- **Inspect before use.** Retire any battery that is swollen, cracked, leaking, or unusually hot, and recycle it at a store drop-off — never in household garbage.

### Corded and Cordless Tools

- Keep motors and vents **free of dust**; blow out vents with compressed air after dusty jobs.
- Wipe tools down after use and store them dry to prevent rust.
- Check cords and triggers for wear; do not use a tool with a damaged cord.
- Keep moving parts lightly lubricated per the manual.

## Blade, Bit & Accessory Care

- **Keep blades clean and sharp.** Remove pitch and resin from saw blades with a dedicated blade cleaner; dull or gummed blades strain the motor and are a safety hazard.
- **Replace, don't force.** Worn blades, drill bits, and abrasives are **consumables** and are not covered under warranty — replace them when cutting slows or burning appears.
- **Store edges protected.** Keep blades in their sleeves and chisels/planes with blade guards to protect both the edge and your hands.
- **Prevent rust** on metal tools with a light coat of machine oil and silica-gel packs in your toolbox.

## Paint & Coatings Storage

- **Seal tightly.** Tap the lid down fully (protect the rim with a cloth) so a skin doesn't form.
- **Store upright** in a cool, dry place that **does not freeze** — most latex/water-based paints are ruined by freezing.
- **Label and date** custom-tinted colours and note the formula so we can re-mix an exact match later.
- **Shelf life:** unopened paint lasts for years; opened latex paint typically lasts **2–5 years** if well sealed, and opened oil-based paint can last longer.
- **Dispose responsibly.** Dried-out or unwanted paint can be dropped off at most stores through provincial paint-recycling programs (see [Store Policies › Environmental and Recycling Programs](store-policies.md)).

## Outdoor Power Equipment

### End-of-Season Winterization

- **Fuel:** either run the equipment dry, or add fuel stabilizer and run it for a few minutes so treated fuel reaches the carburetor. Stale fuel is the most common cause of spring no-starts.
- **Oil:** change the engine oil at the end of the season so contaminants don't sit over winter.
- **Battery:** remove and store equipment batteries indoors per the [battery care](#voltedge--probuilt-lithium-ion-batteries) guidance above.
- **Blades and augers:** clean off debris, sharpen mower blades, and apply a light coat of oil to bare metal.
- **Spark plug:** inspect and replace if worn; a fresh plug makes spring starts easier.
- **Store dry and covered**, off bare concrete where possible.

### Spring Start-Up

- Replace any old fuel, check oil level, reinstall a charged battery, and inspect belts and cables before first use.

## Seasonal Plumbing Care (AquaFlow & General)

### Fall / Winter

- **Drain and shut off outdoor faucets and hoses** before the first hard frost; install insulated faucet covers.
- **Blow out irrigation lines** to prevent freeze damage.
- **Insulate exposed pipes** in unheated areas (garages, crawl spaces) with foam sleeves or heat tape.
- **Know your main shut-off** so you can stop water quickly if a pipe bursts.

### Spring / Summer

- **Check for leaks** at supply lines, hose bibs, and under sinks as things thaw.
- **Test sump pumps** before the spring melt by pouring water into the pit to confirm it cycles.
- **Flush the water heater** annually to clear sediment and maintain efficiency; check the temperature/pressure relief valve.

## General Storage Tips

- Store chemicals, fuels, and flammables per their labels, away from heat and ignition sources, and out of reach of children (see [Safety & Recalls](safety-and-recalls.md)).
- Keep a dry, ventilated space for tools to prevent rust and mildew.
- Retain manuals and receipts; BuildRight Rewards members can also retrieve purchase records from their account for warranty claims.
