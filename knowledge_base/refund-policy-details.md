# Refund Policy Details

## Overview

This page provides the fine print behind BuildRight Hardware refunds: exact timelines by payment method, how partial refunds and restocking fees are calculated, the reasons a refund may be denied, and how to escalate if something goes wrong. For the basics of what gets refunded, see the [Refund Policy](refunds.md). For what can be returned, see the [Returns Policy](returns.md).

## Refund Timelines by Payment Method

Once a return is approved (immediately in store, or after inspection at our returns centre for mailed returns), refunds are issued on the following timelines:

| Payment Method | When Refund Is Issued | When Funds Appear |
|---|---|---|
| Visa / Mastercard / Amex | Same day (in store) or 5–7 business days after inspection (online) | 5–7 business days, depending on your bank |
| Debit card | Same day (in store) or 5–7 business days (online) | 1–5 business days |
| BuildRight Credit Card | Within 5–7 business days | Posts to your next statement |
| Cash (in store) | Immediately | Immediately |
| Cash (online order) | Cheque mailed within 10 business days | Plus Canada Post delivery time |
| BuildRight gift card | Immediately (in store) or 3–5 business days (online) | Balance available immediately on issue |
| BuildRight Rewards credit | Within 3 business days | Restored to your rewards account |
| Financing / Equal Payment Plan | Within 5–7 business days | Credited to your financing balance |

The "when funds appear" timing is controlled by your financial institution and is outside BuildRight's control. Most card refunds land within **5–7 business days** of being issued.

## Why a Card Refund Can Take Longer

- **Weekends and statutory holidays** do not count as business days.
- **Bank processing batches** can add 1–3 days after we release the refund.
- **Expired or replaced cards:** the refund still routes to the same account in most cases; if it cannot, we issue a gift card or merchandise credit.
- **Mailed returns** only start the clock once the item arrives and passes inspection at our returns centre.

## Partial Refunds

A partial refund is issued when you receive less than the full original amount. Common scenarios:

- **Partial return:** you keep some items and return others. You are refunded for the returned items plus their proportional tax.
- **Promo and bundle adjustments:** if a discount required a minimum spend or a bundle that the remaining items no longer meet, the discount is recalculated and the difference may be deducted.
- **Missing components:** returns missing packaging, manuals, chargers, or accessories may be reduced to cover replacement.
- **Reduced resale condition:** items returned opened or used (where allowed) may incur a restocking fee.
- **Non-refundable shipping:** for change-of-mind online returns, original outbound shipping is not refunded.

### Worked Example

You order two items totalling $200 plus 13% HST ($26.00), with a $20 "spend $150, save $20" discount applied across the order, and free shipping. You return one item with a list price of $120.

- Refunded item portion after the proportional discount: about **$108**
- Proportional HST refunded: about **$14.04**
- Because the remaining item ($80) no longer meets the $150 threshold, the $20 discount is removed from it and that amount is not refunded back to you.
- Net refund: approximately **$122.04**, and the kept item is now charged at its full price.

We always show the exact refund breakdown before finalizing.

## Restocking Fees

A restocking fee of up to **15%** of the item price may apply to:

- Opened power tools and electronics returned without a defect claim
- Opened generators, pressure washers, and gas-powered outdoor equipment
- Special-order items we agree to take back (in addition to the 20% non-refundable deposit)

Restocking fees are **waived** when:

- The item is defective, damaged in transit, or shipped in error
- You are a BuildRight Rewards **Pro** or **Contractor** tier member (most categories)
- The item is returned unopened in factory-sealed condition

You will always be told the fee amount before the refund is processed.

## Refund Denials

A refund may be denied when:

- The item shows misuse, modification, or customer-caused damage
- The item is non-returnable (opened paint, custom-mixed product, cut-to-length goods, final sale)
- The return window has lapsed and there is no qualifying defect
- The item is incomplete or is not the item originally sold
- The purchase or return appears fraudulent
- No acceptable proof of purchase is available and the no-receipt limit ($150 per customer per 90 days) is exceeded

If we deny a refund, we will explain the reason and offer to return the item to you at no charge.

## How to Escalate a Refund

If your refund is late, the amount looks wrong, or a request was denied and you would like it reviewed:

1. **Confirm the timeline.** Allow the full 5–7 business days for card refunds before escalating.
2. **Contact the refunds team.** Email refunds@buildright.ca or call 1-800-BUILD-IT with your order number, return confirmation, and the last four digits of the payment card.
3. **Request a supervisor review.** If unresolved within **3 business days**, ask for an escalation to a customer-care supervisor; you will receive a case number.
4. **Provide your bank reference.** For card refunds that we have released but you cannot see, your bank may need the **ARN (Acquirer Reference Number)**, which our team can provide so your bank can locate the deposit.
5. **Final review.** Unresolved cases are reviewed by our resolutions team, which responds within **5 business days** with a final decision.

Keep your return tracking number and any inspection notes; they speed up every step.
