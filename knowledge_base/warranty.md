# Warranty Policy

## Manufacturer Warranty

All products sold at BuildRight Hardware carry the manufacturer's warranty. Warranty periods vary by brand and product type:

## Standard Warranty Periods

| Category | Typical Warranty |
|---|---|
| Mastercraft Hand Tools | Lifetime limited |
| Mastercraft Power Tools | 2 years |
| Kitchen Appliances | 1 year |
| Electrical Products | 1 year |
| Automotive Products | 90 days |
| Cleaning Products | 30 days (defects only) |

## What Is Covered

Manufacturer warranties cover defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This includes:
- Structural failures not caused by misuse
- Electrical component failures in power tools
- Motor failures under normal operating conditions
- Premature finish deterioration (paint and coatings, where applicable)

## What Is Not Covered

Warranties do not cover:
- Damage from misuse, abuse, or accidents
- Normal wear and tear (blades, brushes, belts)
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents) not affecting function
- Modifications or repairs performed by unauthorized parties
- Damage from using the product outside its rated specifications

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. **In-store**: Bring the product and your receipt to any BuildRight Hardware location. Our staff will assess the defect and process the claim.
2. **Online**: Log in, go to "My Orders," select the item, and click "Warranty Claim." Attach photos of the defect.
3. For Mastercraft branded products, you may also contact the manufacturer directly through their website.

## Warranty Claim Resolution

Approved warranty claims will be resolved by:
- **Repair**: The product is repaired at no cost to you.
- **Replacement**: A new or equivalent product is provided.
- **Refund**: If repair or replacement is not available, a full refund is issued.

Most warranty claims are resolved within **10–14 business days**.

## Extended Warranty Options

BuildRight Hardware offers extended protection plans on power tools and kitchen appliances. These plans extend coverage for up to 4 additional years and include accidental damage protection. Ask a store associate or see the product page online for details.
