# Warranty Policy

## Overview

Every product sold at BuildRight Hardware is backed by a manufacturer's warranty against defects in materials and workmanship. Many of our house brands carry warranties that meet or exceed the industry standard. This policy explains warranty periods, what is and is not covered, how to make a claim, and the extended protection plans available at checkout.

Warranty coverage is separate from your right to return an item. For change-of-mind returns, see the [Returns Policy](returns.md).

## Manufacturer Warranty

All products sold at BuildRight Hardware carry the manufacturer's warranty. Warranty periods vary by brand and product type. Our house brands — **Mastercraft**, **ProBuilt**, **IronClad**, **VoltEdge**, and **AquaFlow** — are warranted by BuildRight Hardware directly, so claims can be handled at any store.

## What Is Covered

Manufacturer warranties cover defects in materials and workmanship under normal use. This includes:

- Structural failures not caused by misuse
- Electrical component failures in power tools
- Motor failures under normal operating conditions
- Battery cells that fail to hold a charge within the rated cycle life
- Premature finish deterioration on plumbing fixtures and coatings, where applicable
- Manufacturing defects present on arrival (also covered as a defective item under the [Returns Policy](returns.md))

## What Is Not Covered

Warranties do not cover:

- Damage from misuse, abuse, accidents, or improper installation
- Normal wear and tear on consumable parts (blades, bits, brushes, belts, filters, abrasives)
- Cosmetic damage (scratches, dents) that does not affect function
- Modifications or repairs performed by unauthorized parties
- Damage from using a product outside its rated specifications or voltage
- Damage from lack of required maintenance (see [Product Care & Maintenance](product-care-and-maintenance.md))
- Rust or corrosion caused by improper storage
- Commercial or rental use of products rated for household use (unless purchased on a [Commercial or Pro account](commercial-and-pro-accounts.md))

## How to Make a Warranty Claim

1. **In store:** bring the product and your receipt to any BuildRight Hardware location. Our staff will assess the defect and process the claim. Keep your receipt — BuildRight Rewards members can also retrieve proof of purchase from their account.
2. **Online:** log in at buildright.ca, go to **My Orders**, select the item, and click **Warranty Claim**. Attach clear photos or a short video of the defect.
3. **Manufacturer direct:** for some national brands, you may also contact the manufacturer's warranty line directly. For our house brands (Mastercraft, ProBuilt, IronClad, VoltEdge, AquaFlow), BuildRight handles the claim for you.

### What You Will Need

- Proof of purchase (receipt, order number, or BuildRight Rewards lookup)
- The product, including any detachable parts and accessories
- A description of the problem and when it began
- Photos or video for online claims

## Warranty Claim Resolution

Approved warranty claims are resolved by one of the following, at our discretion:

- **Repair:** the product is repaired at no cost to you.
- **Replacement:** a new or equivalent product is provided.
- **Refund:** if repair or replacement is not available, a refund is issued (prorated for limited-lifetime items based on age and use).

Most warranty claims are assessed within **10–14 business days**. Parts-dependent repairs on appliances and outdoor power equipment may take longer; we will keep you informed and provide an estimated completion date.

## Lifetime Limited Warranty Explained

"Lifetime limited" warranties (such as Mastercraft and IronClad hand tools) cover defects in materials and workmanship for the useful life of the product. They do not cover normal wear, loss, theft, or misuse. To claim, simply bring the defective tool to any store — no receipt is required for hand tools covered by a lifetime warranty, and we will repair or replace it on the spot where stock allows.

## Extended Warranty Options

BuildRight Hardware offers **BuildRight Protection Plans** on power tools, kitchen and major appliances, outdoor power equipment, and electronics.

- Extends coverage for up to **4 additional years** beyond the manufacturer warranty.
- Includes **accidental damage protection** (drops, spills, power surges) on eligible categories.
- Covers parts, labour, and in-home service for major appliances.
- Includes **one free maintenance check** per year on outdoor power equipment plans.
- Fully transferable if you sell or gift the product.
- If we cannot repair a covered product, we replace it — a **no-lemon guarantee** applies after three qualifying repairs.

Plans can be added at checkout (in store or online) within **30 days** of your purchase. Pricing is based on the product category and price. Ask a store associate or see the product page online for details.
