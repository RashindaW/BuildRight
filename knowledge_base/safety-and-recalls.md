# Safety & Recalls

## Overview

BuildRight AI takes product safety seriously. This page explains how we handle product recalls, where to find Safety Data Sheets, which items are age-restricted, our guidance on personal protective equipment, and the rules for handling and shipping hazardous materials. Following product safety information protects you, your household, and your warranty.

## Product Recalls

### How We Handle Recalls

When a manufacturer or Health Canada issues a recall on a product we sell:

1. We **post the recall** on the Product Recalls page at smarthandyman.ca with affected models, lot numbers, and what to do.
2. We **notify customers we can identify**, including BuildRight Rewards members who purchased the item, by email.
3. We **remove affected stock** from shelves and the website immediately.

### What to Do If You Have a Recalled Item

- **Stop using the product** right away if the recall advises it.
- Check the recall notice for the remedy — typically a **free repair, replacement, or full refund**.
- Bring the item (or the affected part) and your proof of purchase to any store, or start a claim online under **My Orders › Report a Problem**.
- Recall remedies are provided **at no cost** and are **not subject to the normal return window** — a recalled item can be returned regardless of how long ago it was purchased.

### Reporting a Safety Concern

If you believe a product is unsafe but is not yet under recall, contact us at safety@smarthandyman.ca or 1-800-BUILD-IT. Serious incidents can also be reported to **Health Canada's Consumer Product Safety** program.

## Safety Data Sheets (SDS)

For chemical and hazardous products (paints, solvents, adhesives, pesticides, cleaners, lubricants):

- **Online:** the SDS is linked on the product page at smarthandyman.ca.
- **In store:** ask any associate; we keep SDS documentation available at the service desk.
- **By request:** email support@smarthandyman.ca with the product name and we will send the current SDS.

Always read the SDS and product label before use, and follow the handling, storage, and disposal instructions.

## Age-Restricted Items

Some products require the purchaser to be **18 or older**. Valid government-issued photo ID may be requested in store or at delivery, and these items are often restricted to **in-store pickup**:

- **Propane tanks**, fuel canisters, and pressurized fuels
- Certain **chemicals and solvents** (some drain cleaners, strong acids/bases, certain pesticides)
- **Powder-actuated tools** and certain high-powered equipment
- Some **aerosols and accelerants**

We do not sell age-restricted items to anyone who cannot provide valid proof of age. See [Store Policies › Age Restrictions](store-policies.md).

## Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)

We strongly recommend appropriate PPE for every project, and we carry a full range:

- **Eye protection** (safety glasses, goggles, face shields) for cutting, grinding, and chemical work
- **Hearing protection** for power tools and loud equipment
- **Respirators and dust masks** for sanding, painting, insulation, and chemical use
- **Gloves** suited to the task (cut-resistant, chemical-resistant, work gloves)
- **Hard hats, hi-vis, and fall protection** for job-site work

In our stores, **PPE is required in designated workshop and demo areas**, and in-store power tool demonstrations are performed by trained associates only (see [Store Policies › Power Tool Demonstrations](store-policies.md)).

## Hazardous Materials Handling and Storage

- **Store flammables and chemicals** in their original containers, tightly closed, away from heat, sparks, and direct sunlight, and out of reach of children and pets.
- **Never mix chemicals** unless the label explicitly directs it (for example, never mix bleach and ammonia).
- **Propane and pressurized cylinders** must be stored upright, outdoors or in a well-ventilated space, never in living areas or hot vehicles, per local regulations.
- **Dispose responsibly** through provincial household hazardous waste and stewardship programs; many stores accept paint, batteries, and electronics for recycling (see [Store Policies › Environmental and Recycling Programs](store-policies.md)).

## Hazardous Materials & Shipping Restrictions

Transport regulations (TDG — Transportation of Dangerous Goods) limit what can be shipped. The following are **in-store pickup only** and cannot be shipped:

- Propane tanks, fuel canisters, and pressurized fuels
- Many **aerosols** (spray paints, lubricants, expanding foam) classified as dangerous goods
- **Flammable liquids** such as solvents, paint thinners, and certain adhesives
- **Corrosives and oxidizers**, and large quantities of pesticides restricted by provincial rules
- Certain **lithium batteries** above size/quantity thresholds when not installed in a product

These restrictions exist to keep carriers, our staff, and your household safe. See [Shipping & Delivery › Delivery Restrictions](shipping-delivery.md).
