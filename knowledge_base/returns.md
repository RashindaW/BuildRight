# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Overview

BuildRight Hardware wants you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with your order, we offer a straightforward return and refund process.

## Return Window

You have **90 days** from the date of purchase to return most items for a full refund or exchange. Items must be in their original condition with original packaging and tags attached.

## Eligible Items

Most products sold at BuildRight Hardware are eligible for return, including:
- Hand tools (unused or defective)
- Power tools (unopened or defective within warranty)
- Hardware and fasteners (unopened original packaging)
- Paint and coatings (unopened cans only)
- Electrical and plumbing supplies (unopened)
- Cleaning products (unopened)
- Seasonal items (within the 90-day window)

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items **cannot** be returned:
- Opened paint cans (mixed or custom-tinted)
- Propane tanks and fuel canisters (for safety reasons)
- Items that have been used, installed, or show signs of wear
- Special-order or custom items
- Items without a receipt or proof of purchase

## How to Return

1. Bring the item to any BuildRight Hardware store location with your original receipt or order confirmation.
2. For online orders, log in to your account, go to "My Orders," and select "Request Return." You will receive a prepaid shipping label via email within 1–2 business days.
3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging where possible.

## Refund Processing

- **In-store returns**: Refunds are processed immediately to your original payment method.
- **Online returns**: Once the item is received and inspected, refunds are issued within **5–7 business days** to the original payment method.
- Cash purchases are refunded in cash (in-store) or by cheque (online, mailed within 10 business days).

## Exchanges

You may exchange any eligible item for a product of equal or greater value. If the replacement item costs more, you pay the difference. If less, the difference is refunded.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrived damaged or is defective, contact us within **30 days** of delivery. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at no cost to you, including free return shipping for online orders.
