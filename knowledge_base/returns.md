# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Overview

BuildRight Hardware wants you to be completely satisfied with every purchase. If a product is not right for your project, your home, or your job site, we offer a straightforward, no-hassle return and exchange process both in store and online. This policy explains what you can return, how long you have, and exactly how to do it.

For questions about how money is returned to you, see our [Refund Policy](refunds.md) and [Refund Policy Details](refund-policy-details.md).

## Return Window

You have **90 days** from the date of purchase to return most items for a full refund or exchange. The purchase date printed on your receipt or order confirmation is the start of the window.

- **Standard items:** 90 days from purchase.
- **Defective or damaged items:** report within **30 days** of delivery for a free replacement or full refund, including free return shipping for online orders (see [Damaged or Defective Items](#damaged-or-defective-items)).
- **Major appliances** (refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, dishwashers): **30 days** from delivery for change-of-mind returns. Defect claims after 30 days are handled under the [Warranty Policy](warranty.md).
- **Holiday purchases:** items bought between November 1 and December 24 may be returned until **January 31** of the following year, even if that exceeds 90 days.
- **BuildRight Rewards members** automatically receive an extended **120-day** return window on most items, since we have your purchase history on file.

Items must be in their original condition with original packaging, manuals, accessories, and any tags attached.

## Eligible Items

Most products sold at BuildRight Hardware are eligible for return, including:

- Hand tools (unused, or defective within warranty)
- Power tools (unopened, or defective within warranty)
- Hardware and fasteners (unopened original packaging)
- Paint and coatings (unopened, factory-sealed cans only)
- Electrical and plumbing supplies (unopened)
- Lighting and fixtures (unopened or uninstalled)
- Cleaning products (unopened)
- Lawn, garden, and seasonal items (within the 90-day window)
- Storage, shelving, and organization products

### Proof of Purchase

A valid proof of purchase is required for all returns. We accept:

- An original or digital BuildRight Hardware receipt
- An online order confirmation or packing slip
- The payment card used (for purchases we can look up electronically)
- Your **BuildRight Rewards** account, which stores up to **24 months** of purchase history

Returns without any proof of purchase may be offered, at our discretion, as a **merchandise credit** at the item's current selling price.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items **cannot** be returned for change of mind. Defective items in these categories are still covered under the [Warranty Policy](warranty.md) where applicable.

- Opened paint cans (custom-tinted or colour-matched to your specification)
- Propane tanks, fuel canisters, and other pressurized fuel products (safety reasons)
- Cut-to-length products (chain, cable, rope, wire, blinds, lumber cut on request)
- Items that have been used, installed, assembled, or show signs of wear
- Special-order and custom-manufactured items (see [Store Policies › Special Orders](store-policies.md))
- Gift cards (non-returnable and non-refundable)
- Hazardous materials once opened (certain adhesives, solvents, pesticides)
- Final-sale and clearance items marked "as-is" or "no returns"
- Items without any acceptable proof of purchase (may qualify for merchandise credit only)

## How to Return

### In Store

1. Bring the item to any BuildRight Hardware store location with your original receipt or order confirmation.
2. Take the item to the Returns & Customer Service desk near the store entrance.
3. Our associate will inspect the item and process your refund or exchange on the spot. No appointment is needed.

You may return online orders in store at no cost — this is the fastest way to get your money back.

### Online

1. Log in to your account at buildright.ca, go to **My Orders**, and select **Request Return**.
2. Choose the items and a reason for the return.
3. You will receive a **prepaid return shipping label** by email within **1–2 business days**.
4. Pack the item securely in its original packaging where possible, attach the label, and drop it at any authorized carrier location.

Online returns that are not the result of a defect or our error may have the standard outbound shipping cost deducted, and a restocking fee may apply to certain categories (see [Refund Policy › Restocking Fees](refunds.md)).

## Refund Processing

- **In-store returns:** refunds are processed immediately to your original payment method.
- **Online returns:** once the item is received and inspected at our returns centre, refunds are issued within **5–7 business days** to the original payment method.
- **Cash purchases:** refunded in cash in store, or by cheque mailed within **10 business days** for online orders.

For a full breakdown of refund timelines by payment method, see [Refund Policy Details](refund-policy-details.md).

## Exchanges

You may exchange any eligible item for a product of equal or greater value.

- If the replacement item costs **more**, you pay the difference.
- If the replacement item costs **less**, the difference is refunded to your original payment method.
- Exchanges are processed immediately in store, with no waiting period for a refund.
- For online orders, the fastest exchange is to return the original item and place a new order for the replacement.

## Damaged or Defective Items

If your item arrived damaged or is defective, contact us within **30 days** of delivery. We will arrange a free replacement or full refund at no cost to you, including free return shipping for online orders.

1. Do not discard the item or its packaging until your claim is resolved.
2. For online orders, take photos of the damage and the shipping box.
3. Start a claim through **My Orders › Report a Problem**, or contact us at 1-800-BUILD-IT.

Visibly damaged deliveries should be refused at the door where possible. For freight and oversized shipments, note any damage on the delivery receipt before signing. See [Shipping & Delivery › Damaged Shipments](shipping-delivery.md) for details.

## Returns Without a Receipt

If you cannot locate a receipt and we are unable to find your purchase electronically or in your BuildRight Rewards history, we may offer a **merchandise credit** for the item's current selling price. A valid government-issued photo ID may be requested to deter fraud, and we limit no-receipt returns to **$150 per customer per 90 days**.
