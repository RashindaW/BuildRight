# Assembly & Installation Services

## Overview

BuildRight AI offers assembly and professional installation so your purchase is ready to use without the hassle. From snapping together a BBQ to installing a new dishwasher or smart thermostat, our trained associates and licensed installer network handle the work. This page covers what's available, what's included, fees, and how to book.

All installation work is performed by trained BuildRight associates or vetted, licensed, and insured contractors in our installer network.

## In-Store and Curbside Assembly

For many products you can skip the flat-pack experience entirely.

- **BBQs and gas grills:** assembled and leak-tested before pickup. Free on most models; a flat **$40** assembly fee applies to premium and built-in units.
- **Wheelbarrows, garden carts, and spreaders:** assembled free at the service desk on request.
- **Furniture, shelving, and storage units:** in-store assembly available from **$25**, depending on complexity.
- **Bicycles and ride-on toys:** tuned and assembled from **$30**.

Ask any associate, or select **Add Assembly** on the product page when ordering online for pickup.

## In-Home Assembly

For larger items, our team can assemble at your home.

- Booked at checkout or by calling 1-800-BUILD-IT.
- Pricing starts at **$60** and is quoted based on the item and complexity before you confirm.
- Includes unboxing, full assembly, a function check, and removal of all packaging.

## Professional Installation

We provide professional installation for the categories below. All installations include a workmanship guarantee and haul-away of packaging.

- **Dishwashers:** from **$129** — includes connection to existing water, drain, and power, level and secure, and a leak test.
- **Over-the-range microwaves:** from **$149** — includes mounting and venting to existing ductwork.
- **Laundry (washers/dryers):** from **$99** — includes hookup to existing connections and a test cycle. Gas dryer connections require a licensed gas fitter (additional fee).

- **Refrigerators with water/ice:** from **$119** — includes water line connection to an existing, accessible shut-off.

Old-appliance **haul-away** is available for **$30** per item where local regulations permit.

## What's Included

Every professional installation includes:

- An appointment with a confirmed arrival window (typically 3–4 hours)
- Installation by a trained associate or licensed contractor
- A function and safety check
- Removal and disposal of all packaging
- A **1-year workmanship guarantee** on the installation labour (separate from the product [Warranty](warranty.md))

## What's Not Included

Installation quotes assume standard, code-compliant existing connections. The following are extra or require a separate licensed trade and may need a quote on site:

- New plumbing, gas, or electrical lines, and any permit fees
- Modifications to cabinetry, countertops, flooring, or framing
- Work that requires bringing existing systems up to current code
- Removal of non-standard or built-in old units beyond standard haul-away

If the installer finds extra work is required, they will pause and provide a written quote before continuing.

## Booking and Rescheduling

1. **Book** at checkout (in store or online) by adding the installation service, or call 1-800-BUILD-IT.
2. **Confirm** your appointment window by email or SMS.
3. **Reschedule or cancel** at no charge up to **24 hours** before your appointment. Late cancellations may incur a **$50** trip fee.
4. **In-home prep:** clear the work area and ensure access to shut-offs and outlets so the installer can work safely.
