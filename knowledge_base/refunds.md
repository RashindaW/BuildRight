# Refund Policy

## Overview

BuildRight AI issues refunds for eligible returned items promptly and to the original payment method wherever possible. This policy explains how refunds are calculated, when they are issued, and the circumstances under which a refund may be reduced or denied.

For details on which items can be returned and how to start a return, see our [Returns Policy](returns.md). For payment-method-specific timelines, partial refunds, and escalation steps, see [Refund Policy Details](refund-policy-details.md).

## Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at purchase:

| Original Payment | Refund Method | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| Credit Card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) | Same card | 5–7 business days |
| Debit Card | Same card | 5–7 business days |
| BuildRight Credit Card | Same account | 5–7 business days |
| Cash (in-store return) | Cash on the spot | Immediate |
| Cash (online order) | Cheque mailed | Within 10 business days |
| Gift Card | New gift card or balance reload | Immediate in store; 3–5 business days online |
| BuildRight Rewards credit | Credit restored to rewards account | Within 3 business days |
| Financing / Equal Payment Plan | Credit to financing balance | 5–7 business days |

If your original card is expired, cancelled, or lost, we can issue the refund to a replacement card on the same account, or as a **merchandise credit** or **gift card** if the account is closed.

## What Gets Refunded

- **Item price:** the actual price you paid, after any discounts or promotions applied to that item.
- **Sales tax (GST/HST/PST):** refunded in full proportionally to the returned items.
- **Outbound shipping:** refunded only when the return is the result of a defect, damage, or a BuildRight error. For change-of-mind online returns, original shipping charges are non-refundable.
- **Return shipping:** free (prepaid label) for defects, damage, or our error; otherwise the cost of the prepaid label may be deducted from the refund.

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued when:

- Only some items in an order are returned (you are refunded for the returned items only).
- An item is returned in a condition that reduces its resale value (a restocking fee may apply — up to **15%**).
- An item is returned without its original packaging, accessories, or manuals (a deduction may apply to cover replacement).
- A promotional discount or bundle was applied to the original order; the refund reflects the price you actually paid, and returning part of a bundle may forfeit the bundle discount.
- A "buy more, save more" or gift-with-purchase threshold is no longer met after the return (the value of the free gift or tiered discount may be deducted if not returned).

## Restocking Fees

A restocking fee of up to **15%** may be applied to:

- Opened power tools and electronics returned without a valid defect claim.
- Opened generators, pressure washers, and gas-powered outdoor equipment.
- Special-order items that we agree to accept back (in addition to the non-refundable deposit).

We will always inform you of any restocking fee **before** processing your refund, and the fee is **waived** when the return is due to a defect, damage, or a BuildRight error. BuildRight Rewards members in the **Pro** and **Contractor** tiers have restocking fees waived on most categories.

## Refund Denials

We reserve the right to deny a refund if:

- The item shows signs of misuse, modification, abuse, or customer-caused damage.
- The item was listed as non-returnable (e.g., opened paint, custom-mixed product, cut-to-length goods).
- The return window has expired and the item has no qualifying defect.
- The item is missing, incomplete, or not the item originally purchased.
- We have reasonable grounds to believe the purchase or return is fraudulent.
- No acceptable proof of purchase can be provided and the item exceeds our no-receipt limit.

If a refund is denied, we will explain why and, where appropriate, return the item to you at no charge.

## Exchanges as an Alternative

If you prefer not to wait for a refund, we are happy to offer an immediate exchange for an item of equal or greater value. Exchanges are processed on the spot in store. See our [Returns Policy](returns.md) for exchange details.

## Sales Tax and Province-Specific Notes

Refunded sales tax is returned at the same rate it was charged, based on the original purchase. Because tax rates differ by province (for example, GST only in Alberta versus HST in Ontario), the refunded tax amount matches what appeared on your original receipt regardless of where you submit the return.
