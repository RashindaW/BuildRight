# Refund Policy

## Overview

BuildRight Hardware issues refunds for eligible returned items. For a full explanation of which items can be returned and how to start a return, see our [Returns Policy](returns.md).

## Refund Methods

Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at purchase:

| Original Payment | Refund Method |
|---|---|
| Credit / Debit Card | Same card, 5–7 business days |
| Cash (in-store) | Cash on the spot |
| Cash (online order) | Cheque mailed within 10 business days |
| Gift Card | New gift card issued for the same value |
| BuildRight Rewards credit | Credit restored to your rewards account |

## Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued when:
- Only some items in an order are returned.
- An item is returned in a condition that reduces its resale value (restocking fee may apply — up to 15%).
- A promotional discount was applied to the original order; the refunded amount reflects the price you actually paid.

## Restocking Fees

A restocking fee of up to 15% may be applied to opened power tools and electronics returned without a defect claim. We will inform you of any fee before processing your refund.

## Refund Denials

We reserve the right to deny a refund if:
- The item shows signs of misuse, modification, or damage caused by the customer.
- The item was listed as non-returnable (e.g., opened paint, custom-mixed product).
- The return window has expired and the item has no defect.
- We have reason to believe the purchase was fraudulent.

## Exchanges as an Alternative

If you prefer not to wait for a refund, we are happy to offer an immediate exchange for an item of equal or greater value. See our [Returns Policy](returns.md) for exchange details.

## Contact for Refund Issues

If your refund is not received within the stated timeframe, contact us:
- **Online chat** (during store hours)
- **Email**: refunds@buildright.ca
- **Phone**: 1-800-BUILD-IT

Please have your order number and original payment method ready when contacting us.
