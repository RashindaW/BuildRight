# Financing & Credit

## Overview

The **BuildRight Credit Card** and our financing plans help you spread the cost of larger projects — appliances, tool collections, renovations, and seasonal equipment — over time. This page explains the card, the financing options, eligibility, interest, and how to apply.

Credit is provided through our banking partner and is subject to approval. Terms below are typical; the exact rate, fees, and terms are disclosed in your cardholder agreement at the time of approval.

## The BuildRight Credit Card

A retail credit card you can use across all BuildRight AI stores and at smarthandyman.ca.

### Cardholder Benefits

- **Bonus BuildRight Rewards:** earn **2 points per $1** on BuildRight purchases made with the card (double the standard rate) — see the [Rewards Program](rewards-program.md).
- **Access to special financing** offers (equal-payment and deferred plans) on qualifying purchases.
- **Exclusive cardholder events** and early access to select sales.
- **No annual fee.**
- Digital statements and account management in your smarthandyman.ca account.

### Rates and Fees

- **No annual fee.**
- A standard variable purchase **APR** applies to balances that are not on a promotional financing plan; the exact rate is shown in your cardholder agreement.
- Cash-advance and late-payment fees may apply as disclosed in your agreement.
- Paying your full statement balance by the due date each month means standard purchases incur no interest.

## Financing Plans

Promotional financing is available on qualifying purchases, typically **$299 and over**, when you pay with the BuildRight Credit Card.

### Equal Payment Plans (No-Interest)

- Available in **6, 12, 18, or 24-month** terms on qualifying purchases.
- The purchase is divided into **equal monthly payments** with **0% interest** when every payment is made on time and the balance is paid by the end of the term.
- A one-time **administration fee** may apply (disclosed at checkout).
- Example: a **$1,200** purchase on a 12-month equal-payment plan is about **$100/month**, interest-free, plus any stated admin fee.

### Deferred Payment Plans ("Don't Pay")

- Available on select promotions (for example, **"Don't Pay for 12 Months"**).
- **No payments and no interest** during the deferral period.
- If the full balance is paid before the deferral ends, you pay **no interest**.
- If a balance remains at the end of the deferral period, **accrued interest** from the purchase date may be charged, so we recommend paying it off before the period ends.

### Choosing a Plan

- Equal-payment plans are best if you prefer predictable monthly amounts with no interest.
- Deferred plans are best if you expect to pay the full amount before the term ends.
- Plan availability varies by promotion and purchase amount and is shown at checkout.

## Eligibility

To apply for the BuildRight Credit Card you must:

- Be the **age of majority** in your province or territory
- Be a **Canadian resident** with a valid address
- Have a valid government-issued ID and a Social Insurance Number (used for identity and credit checks)
- Pass our banking partner's credit review

Approval, your credit limit, and your rate are determined by the lender based on your credit profile.

## How to Apply

1. **In store:** apply at any service desk; instant decisions are common, and approved applicants can often use the card the same day.
2. **Online:** apply at smarthandyman.ca — the application takes a few minutes and is secured with SSL encryption.
3. **At checkout:** ask an associate to apply your approved card and select a financing plan on a qualifying purchase.

## Managing Your Account

- View statements, make payments, and set up autopay in your smarthandyman.ca account or through our banking partner's portal.
- Set up **automatic payments** to avoid missing an equal-payment or deferred-plan deadline.
- Lost or stolen card? Report it immediately using the number on the back of your card or in your online account.

## Financing and Returns

If you return an item purchased on a financing plan, the refund is **credited to your financing balance**, which reduces or clears the plan. If the plan is paid off, the balance is refunded per the [Refund Policy Details](refund-policy-details.md). Admin fees already charged on a financing plan are generally non-refundable.
