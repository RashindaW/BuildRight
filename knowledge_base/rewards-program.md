# BuildRight Rewards Program

## Overview

**BuildRight Rewards** is our free loyalty program. Earn points on nearly every purchase, redeem them for money off future orders, and unlock tier benefits as you spend. Membership also stores your purchase history, which makes returns and warranty claims easier. This page covers earning, redemption, tiers, bonus events, expiry, enrollment, and exclusions.

Membership is **free** and there is no annual fee.

## How to Enroll

- **Online:** sign up at buildright.ca in a couple of minutes.
- **In store:** ask any associate at checkout or the service desk.
- **In the app:** create your account and add your digital membership card.

After enrolling, give your phone number or scan your membership card/app at checkout (in store) or sign in (online) to earn and track points.

## Earning Points

- Earn **1 point per $1 spent** on qualifying purchases (pre-tax).
- Earn **2 points per $1** when you pay with the [BuildRight Credit Card](financing-and-credit.md).
- Points post to your account within **24–48 hours** of purchase, or once an order ships for online orders.
- Points are earned on the amount you actually pay after discounts and reward credits, including on [price-matched](price-match.md) items.
- Services like [Assembly & Installation](assembly-and-installation.md) and [Protection Plans](warranty.md) earn points too.

## Redeeming Points

- **500 points = $5** in reward credit.
- Redeem in **500-point ($5)** increments; for example, **2,000 points = $20**.
- Apply reward credit at checkout **in store or online** — choose how much to redeem on each order.
- Reward credits can be combined with most promotions and used alongside any payment method.
- Unused credit stays in your account for next time.

### Redemption Exclusions

Reward credits and points **cannot** be applied to:

- **Gift card** purchases
- **Propane** tank purchases or exchanges
- Payments toward a **BuildRight Credit Card** balance

## Membership Tiers

Your tier is based on your trailing **12-month** qualifying spend and unlocks added benefits:

| Tier | 12-Month Spend | Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Member** | $0–$1,499 | 1 pt/$1, members-only offers, extended 120-day returns |
| **Pro** | $1,500–$4,999 | 1.25 pts/$1, restocking fees waived on most categories, early sale access |
| **Contractor** | $5,000+ | 1.5 pts/$1, restocking fees waived, priority support, exclusive bonus events |

Tiers are reviewed monthly. Pairing BuildRight Rewards with a [Commercial or Pro account](commercial-and-pro-accounts.md) is ideal for trades and businesses.

## Bonus Point Events

- **Bonus events** (for example, "5x points weekends" or category multipliers on tools, paint, or seasonal goods) are announced in store and by email.
- **Welcome bonus:** new members can earn bonus points on their first qualifying purchase during enrollment promotions.
- **Birthday and anniversary** bonuses are offered to members with a complete profile.
- Bonus multipliers apply automatically when you're signed in or scan your membership at checkout.

## Point Expiry

- Points **do not expire** as long as your account has **qualifying activity within any 12-month period** (a purchase or a redemption counts).
- If your account is inactive for **12 consecutive months**, accumulated points may be forfeited. Any activity resets the 12-month clock.
- We notify members by email before points are at risk of expiring.

## Returns and Points

- If you **return** an item, the points earned on it are **reversed** from your account.
- If you redeemed reward credit on an order and then return items, the redeemed credit is **restored** to your rewards account per the [Refund Policy](refunds.md).

## Managing Your Account

- View your **points balance, tier, and history** in your buildright.ca account or the app.
- Update your contact preferences to receive bonus-event and expiry notifications.
- One membership per person; points are **not transferable** between accounts and have **no cash value** except as reward credit.

## Program Terms

BuildRight Hardware may adjust earning rates, redemption values, tiers, and benefits with reasonable notice. Points and reward credits have no cash value, cannot be sold, and are void where prohibited. Full terms are posted at buildright.ca.
