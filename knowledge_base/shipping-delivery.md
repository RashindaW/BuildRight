# Shipping & Delivery

## Delivery Areas

BuildRight Hardware ships to all provinces and territories in Canada. We do not currently ship outside of Canada.

## Shipping Options and Rates

### Standard Shipping
- **Cost**: $7.99 flat rate, or **free on orders over $49**
- **Estimated delivery**: 5–8 business days
- Available for most items under 30 kg

### Express Shipping
- **Cost**: $14.99 flat rate
- **Estimated delivery**: 2–3 business days
- Available for most items under 30 kg

### Heavy & Oversized Items
- Items over 30 kg or with large dimensions are subject to a freight surcharge calculated at checkout.
- Estimated delivery: 7–14 business days
- Freight deliveries are curbside only (customer is responsible for moving the item inside).

## Free In-Store Pickup

Most orders qualify for **free in-store pickup**, available as soon as same-day (for orders placed before 2 PM). You will receive an email or SMS notification when your order is ready. Orders are held for 7 days.

## Processing Time

- Orders placed before **2 PM local time on business days** are processed same-day.
- Orders placed after 2 PM or on weekends/holidays are processed the next business day.
- Custom or special-order items may require 3–5 additional business days.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can track your order through our website under "My Orders" or via the carrier's website directly.

## Delivery Restrictions

The following items **cannot be shipped** and are available for in-store pickup only:
- Propane tanks and fuel canisters
- Items classified as dangerous goods (certain aerosols and adhesives)

## Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives damaged, do not accept the delivery if visibly damaged at the time of delivery. Contact us within **5 business days** of receiving a damaged package. We will arrange a free replacement at no cost to you.
