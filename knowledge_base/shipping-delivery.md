# Shipping & Delivery

## Overview

BuildRight AI delivers everything from a single box of screws to a full appliance suite across Canada. This page explains our shipping options, rates, delivery timelines, and what to do if something goes wrong with a shipment.

To track an existing order or change a delivery, see [Order Tracking & Changes](order-tracking-and-changes.md).

## Delivery Areas

BuildRight AI ships to all **provinces and territories in Canada**. We do not currently ship outside of Canada.

- Remote and northern postal codes (including parts of the Territories and remote Indigenous communities) may require additional transit time and, for heavy items, an additional remote-area surcharge shown at checkout.
- Some items are restricted from shipping to certain regions due to provincial regulations (see [Delivery Restrictions](#delivery-restrictions)).

## Shipping Options and Rates

All rates are in **CAD** and shown before you complete checkout.

### Standard Shipping

- **Cost:** $7.99 flat rate, or **free on orders over $49**
- **Estimated delivery:** 5–8 business days
- Available for most items under 30 kg

### Express Shipping

- **Cost:** $14.99 flat rate
- **Estimated delivery:** 2–3 business days
- Available for most items under 30 kg
- Order by **2 PM local time** on a business day to ship the same day

### Heavy & Oversized Items

- Items over 30 kg or with large dimensions are subject to a **freight surcharge** calculated at checkout.
- **Estimated delivery:** 7–14 business days
- Freight deliveries are **curbside only** — the customer is responsible for moving the item inside.
- For an additional fee, **room-of-choice delivery** and **haul-away of old appliances** are available on major appliances (see [Major Appliance Delivery](#major-appliance-delivery)).

## Free In-Store Pickup

Most orders qualify for **free in-store pickup**, available as soon as **same-day** for orders placed before **2 PM** local time.

- You will receive an email or SMS notification when your order is ready.
- Orders are held for **7 days**; after that, uncollected orders are cancelled and refunded.
- Bring your order confirmation and a piece of photo ID. For someone else to pick up your order, add them as an **alternate pickup person** in your order details.
- **Curbside pickup** is available at most locations — park in a designated spot and let us know you have arrived through the link in your ready notification.

## Major Appliance Delivery

For refrigerators, washers, dryers, ranges, and dishwashers:

- **Scheduled delivery** with a 4-hour window, booked at checkout or by phone.
- **Basic connection** of the appliance is available for an additional fee (excludes gas lines and electrical that require a licensed trade).
- **Haul-away** of your old appliance is available for a fee where local regulations allow.
- Professional installation is available through our [Assembly & Installation](assembly-and-installation.md) service.

## Processing Time

- Orders placed before **2 PM local time on business days** are processed same-day.
- Orders placed after 2 PM, or on weekends and statutory holidays, are processed the next business day.
- Custom or special-order items may require **3–5 additional business days** before they ship (see [Store Policies › Special Orders](store-policies.md)).
- During major sale events and the holiday season, processing may take 1–2 additional business days.

## Statutory Holidays

We do not ship or deliver on Canadian statutory holidays (for example, New Year's Day, Good Friday, Canada Day, Labour Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day). Orders placed on a holiday are processed the next business day. Observed holidays vary by province, which can affect regional delivery times.

## Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number by email. You can track your order through smarthandyman.ca under **My Orders**, or via the carrier's website directly. See [Order Tracking & Changes](order-tracking-and-changes.md) for help with delays, address changes, and missing items.

## Delivery Restrictions

The following items **cannot be shipped** and are available for **in-store pickup only**:

- Propane tanks, fuel canisters, and other pressurized fuel products
- Items classified as dangerous goods (certain aerosols, solvents, and adhesives)
- Large pesticide and chemical quantities restricted by provincial regulation
- Some firearms-adjacent accessories and age-restricted items (ID required at pickup)

See [Safety & Recalls › Hazardous Materials & Shipping Restrictions](safety-and-recalls.md) for the full list and the reasons behind these limits.

## Damaged Shipments

If your order arrives damaged:

1. **Refuse the delivery** if the damage is visible at the door, where possible.
2. For freight and appliance deliveries, **note the damage on the delivery receipt** before signing.
3. Contact us within **5 business days** of receiving a damaged package, with photos of the item and the box.

We will arrange a free replacement or refund at no cost to you. Do not discard the packaging until the claim is resolved. For defective (not shipping-damaged) items, see the [Returns Policy › Damaged or Defective Items](returns.md).

## Lost or Delayed Shipments

If tracking shows no movement for **5 business days**, or a delivery is marked delivered but you have not received it, contact us at 1-800-BUILD-IT or support@smarthandyman.ca. We will open a carrier investigation and, if the package cannot be located, send a replacement or issue a full refund.
