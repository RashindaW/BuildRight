# Commercial & Pro Accounts

## Overview

BuildRight AI supports contractors, trades, builders, property managers, and businesses with dedicated accounts built for volume, speed, and predictable billing. Commercial and Pro accounts unlock volume pricing, a dedicated rep, net-30 terms, priority processing, and bulk/special-order support. This page explains the tiers, benefits, and how to apply.

## Account Tiers

| Tier | Who It's For | Key Benefits |
|---|---|---|
| **Pro** | Independent tradespeople and small crews | Volume pricing, dedicated checkout lane, restocking fees waived on most categories, BuildRight Rewards Pro tier |
| **Commercial** | Established contractors and businesses | Everything in Pro, plus net-30 terms, a dedicated account rep, bulk/special-order desk |
| **Enterprise** | Multi-site builders, property managers, large fleets | Everything in Commercial, plus custom contract pricing, consolidated multi-site billing, and a national account manager |

You can start with a Pro account and upgrade as your purchasing grows.

## Volume Pricing

- **Tiered contract pricing** on the materials and categories you buy most.
- **Quantity break pricing** on bulk purchases of fasteners, lumber, fixtures, electrical, and more.
- **Quote desk** for project-based pricing on large or recurring orders.
- Pricing is honoured **in store and online** when you sign in to your account.

## Net-30 and Billing Terms

- **Net-30 terms** available on approved Commercial and Enterprise accounts — buy now and pay within 30 days of invoice.
- **Consolidated monthly statements** instead of per-transaction receipts.
- **Purchase orders (POs)** and authorized-buyer lists supported, so your crew can purchase against the account with controls you set.
- **Per-buyer and per-job spending limits** available on Enterprise accounts.
- Credit limits and terms are set during account approval and subject to a credit review.

## Dedicated Account Reps

- **Commercial and Enterprise** accounts are assigned a **dedicated rep** who knows your business.
- Your rep helps with quotes, sourcing, special orders, returns, and account setup.
- **Enterprise** accounts receive a **national account manager** for multi-site coordination.

## Priority Processing and Fulfillment

- **Priority order processing** so your orders move to the front of the queue.
- **Dedicated Pro checkout lanes** at most stores to get you back to the job site faster.
- **Will-call and contractor pickup** areas for fast loading.
- **Job-site delivery** scheduling, including bulk and freight, coordinated through your rep (delivery fees per the [Shipping & Delivery](shipping-delivery.md) policy, with account rates where applicable).

## Bulk and Special Orders

- **Special-order support** for products and quantities beyond regular stock; your rep manages lead times and updates.
- **Bulk ordering** with quantity pricing and staged delivery to match your project schedule.
- Standard special-order terms (lead time and deposit) may be adjusted for established accounts — see [Store Policies › Special Orders](store-policies.md).

## Contractor Benefits

- **Restocking fees waived** on most categories for Pro and above (see [Refund Policy](refunds.md)).
- **Extended return window** and simplified returns tied to your account purchase history.
- **BuildRight Rewards Pro/Contractor tiers** with accelerated earning and bonus events — see the [Rewards Program](rewards-program.md).
- **Bulk gift card programs** for employee and customer incentives — see [Gift Cards](gift-cards.md).
- **Tax-exempt purchasing** supported where you provide valid provincial exemption documentation.
- **Financing** options through the [BuildRight Credit Card](financing-and-credit.md) for qualifying business purchases.

## Eligibility and How to Apply

To open a Commercial or Pro account you typically need:

- A valid **business name** and, for net-30 terms, business registration details
- A **GST/HST number** (and any provincial tax/exemption documentation if claiming exemption)
- Authorized-buyer details for anyone who will purchase on the account
- Consent to a **credit review** for net-30 terms

### How to Apply

1. **Online:** complete the Commercial/Pro account application at buildright.ca.
2. **In store:** ask at the Pro desk or service desk; staff can start your application.
3. **By phone:** call 1-800-BUILD-IT and ask for commercial sales.

Pro accounts are often approved quickly; Commercial and Enterprise accounts with net-30 terms require a short credit review.
